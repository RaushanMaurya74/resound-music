## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes True Shuffle different from standard shuffle?
Standard streaming apps use clustered pseudo-random algorithms that prioritize recently played songs, popular hits, or tracks with lower licensing fees. Resound uses the Fisher-Yates array permutation algorithm, which treats every track on your playlist with equal mathematical probability. Every song plays exactly once.

### Are lyrics really free?
Yes. Synchronized lyrics are an essential accessibility and enjoyment feature. We do not cap how many songs you can view lyrics for, and we do not put lyrics behind a paywall.

### Can I import my existing playlists?
Yes. You can import playlists via CSV or share links. Your track names and artists will be mapped to our streaming catalog within seconds.

### Does Resound include podcasts or audiobooks?
No. Resound is exclusively designed for music. If you want podcasts, there are already dedicated podcast players available. We focus entirely on delivering the best music listening experience possible.

### How does cancellation work?
You can cancel your subscription with a single click in your account settings. There are no survey questionnaires, phone calls, or retention traps.
